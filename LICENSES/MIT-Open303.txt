The ACID engine's DSP (firmware/src/bass303.c, bass303.h, fastmath.h) is a C99 / single-precision / no-libm port of
Open303 by Robin Schmidt (RS-MET, https://www.rs-met.com, https://github.com/RobinSchmidt/Open303): the signal path and
the constants of its getSample() (pitch slew, the decay envelope and its two RC followers, the measured env-mod mapping, the
TB_303-mode TeeBeeFilter after mystran and kunn, its feedback highpass, the amp envelope, the 200 Hz de-clicker and the
allpass / highpass / notch after the filter). It reached Felucca by way of fm1-x0x's dsp/bass303.c (GPL-3.0-only; itself
after schwung-303, GPL-3.0, with the Devilfish ranges after jc303 by midilab and the RAT drive after davemollen/dm-Rat,
GPL-3.0). The ported files stay under the MIT licence below for the Open303 parts; the rest of Felucca, the x0x port and the
RAT drive model are GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE).

The licence of Open303 follows: the standard MIT text, with Robin Schmidt's name as the donor's headers give it. Upstream's
own LICENSE file also carries a year; this tree could not read that file (offline), so none is written here -- add it from
upstream before a release.
